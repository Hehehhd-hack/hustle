// 03 — The Playbook

Zero to paid.

The universal 5-step sequence that works for almost any hustle on this list.

1

Pick one. Not three.

Scattered effort kills momentum. Choose the hustle that matches your current skills and available hours. Commit for 30 days minimum before judging results.

2

Build a 3-sample portfolio.

Before you ask anyone for money, make three free things. A logo set. A short edit. A landing page. Samples convert 10x better than promises.

3

Find your first 3 clients locally.

Your first customers are within 5 miles. Local businesses, family friends, classmates' parents. Face-to-face beats cold DMs when you have no reviews.

4

Charge more than feels comfortable.

Teens chronically underprice. Double your first instinct. If nobody flinches, you're still too cheap. Raise rates every 5 clients until someone says no.

5

Systemize or stagnate.

Once you have 5+ clients, build templates, checklists, and repeatable processes. The hustle becomes a business when it runs without reinventing the wheel each time.
